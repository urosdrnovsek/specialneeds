# Codebase and UX review — November 2026

This review covers the whole app as a user meets it (home, support areas, course catalogue, course pages, play screens on desktop and phone), the game designs, the illustrations, and the code underneath. Part 1 lists what was changed in this pass. Part 2 lists what is still wrong, in priority order, with a recommended path.

## Part 1 — Changed in this pass

### Bugs fixed

| Problem | Effect | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| The dev server (`scripts/serve.cjs`) served `.svg` as `application/octet-stream` | All 1,136 vocabulary pictures showed as broken images ("bag", "cup") on course pages and in games when the app was run locally | Added SVG/JSON/manifest MIME types |
| Opening or reloading `#courses` showed the home page | A bookmarked or shared "Courses" link never worked | The legacy loader in `skill-library.js` no longer claims studio routes (`#courses`, `#practice`, `#review`, `#device`) |
| Breadcrumb items were spread across the full page width | "Find support / Courses / Guided course" looked like three unrelated labels | Breadcrumb is now a compact left-aligned trail |
| Home support-card pictures were letterboxed inside their frames | Cards looked unfinished, with white bands at the sides | Art area now uses the illustration's native 5:3 ratio |

### Play screen redesign (affects every game)

Before: the child's choices usually sat **below the fold** on a laptop and always on a phone. Above the game there were a 91px header, a 90px course bar, a separate "← Back to the course" row, a title and an instruction. On phones the course bar broke into a word-per-line column with an overlapping checkbox, and the bottom tab bar sat under the child's fingers.

Now (`dist/design-system.css`, `dist/design-shell.js`):
- A compact header during play, and no footer.
- One course bar: course name, "Activity n of N", a **progress meter**, Pause, Finish for now, and the automatic-progression checkbox shown as a real **switch**.
- **One exit.** Inside a course the engine's duplicate "← Back to the course" is hidden, so "Finish for now" is the single way out. Outside a course the engine's own back button is unchanged.
- Child-facing game titles use one sans style. Before, they mixed Georgia, Arial and system fonts depending on which engine drew the game.
- On phones inside a course, the bottom navigation is hidden. Automatic progression, untimed play, keyboard and tap alternatives are untouched.
- A skip link for keyboard and screen-reader users; one focus-ring style across the app.

### Games that had no point

Two calm-library activities were the retired pattern AGENTS.md forbids. The child picked a picture, was told "there is no right answer", and nothing used the choice:

- **Here Around Me** is now an I-spy game. The child chooses something to spy, finds it in a room picture (by tap, keyboard or dragging it into the basket), and it leaves the room and appears in the noticing basket. A wrong pick names the object ("That is the lamp. Where is the book?"). Three finds fill the basket, and the game ends with a prompt to spy a real one nearby.
- **A Slow Little Look** is now a close-up game. The child chooses apple, butterfly or spoon, then matches three magnified close-ups to it. Wrong close-ups say which picture they belong to, each found detail is circled on the picture, and the game ends after three details.

"Choose My Pause" stays a choice screen. There the choice *is* the purpose: communicating a preferred kind of break.

### Course catalogue

The 46 courses were one flat wall of cards with repeated pictures. They are now grouped under skill headings, with one-tap skill chips. Choosing a skill in the dropdown applies immediately; it no longer needs "Find courses".

### Home illustration

The hero showed a child beside an oversized planter, with a watering can floating in mid-air. It is replaced by a picture of what the product does: a duck card, two matching apple cards joined and ticked, and "c a t" letter tiles. It is built from the shared picture set, so no new figure anatomy was introduced.

### Website readiness

The site now has a meta description, Open Graph tags, an SVG favicon, a web manifest with icon, a `<noscript>` message, and a descriptive `<title>`.

### Verification

- New: `npm run test:redesign` (`tests/redesign-browser.cjs`). It checks the `#courses` deep link, grouping and filtering, play-mode classes, the progress meter, the single exit, that choices are visible without scrolling at 1280×850, the new hero, both new games through tap, keyboard and drag, wrong-answer behaviour, completion, and no horizontal overflow on a 390px phone.
- Re-run and passing: `test:purposeful`, `test:art` (`art-contacts-dom`, `activity-art-dom`), `test:studio`, `test:device` (including blocked and corrupt storage), `test:reading`, `test:home`, `npm run check`, plus `artwork`, `natural-scenes`, `vocabulary-assets`, `live-loading`, `helpful-hands`, `reading-browser`.
- **Visual review coverage** (screenshots looked at by a person): home, the courses page, one course page, and 22 sampled activities at 1100–1280px; seven key screens on an emulated iPhone 13; every step of both new games. This is separate from the automated rendering checks above.
- **Not verified:** physical touch devices (iOS/iPadOS/Android) and real screen readers. Drag was tested with Chromium mouse events only.

## Part 2 — Findings, in priority order

Items 1–7 were worked through in a second pass; see Part 3 for what changed. Items 8–9 remain open.

### 1. Most of the legacy browser test suites were already failing (high)
At the starting commit, 19 of 24 Playwright suites fail (`reading-browser` fails intermittently; it passed in another run). Most fail before reaching their real checks. They wait for old UI hooks such as `[data-course="visual"]` or `[data-home-view="skills"]`, or for counts the current home no longer renders. They include `startup`, `browser`, `useful-courses`, `course-flow`, `touch-play`, `quick-play`, `calm-garden`, `worlds`, `vocabulary-library` and `purposeful-direct-browser`; only `helpful-hands`, `artwork`, `natural-scenes`, `vocabulary-assets` and `live-loading` pass. `npm test` therefore cannot pass, and regressions in those areas go unnoticed. **Recommendation:** update each suite's entry point to the studio routes (`Studio.go('#courses')`, `skillLaunch(id, i)`), or retire the ones that only test the retired library home. Do this before any larger refactor.

### 2. Architecture: 60 scripts that patch each other (high)
`index.html` loads 60 classic scripts and 21 stylesheets in a fixed order. Later files capture and replace earlier globals: `const old={title:skillTitle,…}`, `libraryHome=function(){calmLibraryBefore();…}`, and `wrap('finish', finish)` in `course-flow.js`. There are at least 16 such override chains. The result:
- Behaviour depends on load order. Several layers re-decorate the same DOM through MutationObservers (`course-flow`, `quick-play`, `course-drag`, `skill-library`, and now `design-shell`).
- Code is written as single 2,000-character lines, so diffs and reviews are close to unreadable.
- Three engines draw the same "choose a picture" task with different markup.

**Recommendation:** move to ES modules with one router and one game interface (`render(state)`, `actions`, `isComplete`, `adultNotes`). Port one engine at a time behind that interface; start with `calm`, which is small and now well tested. Format the code with Prettier first, a no-behaviour change that makes everything else reviewable.

### 3. No single visual system (medium-high)
The CSS has 718 distinct hex colours across 21 files and three conflicting `--ink` definitions. It names fonts that are never loaded (DM Sans, Manrope), and mixes Georgia, Arial and system fonts. `design-system.css` now sets tokens and the shared layout; the next step is to replace hard-coded colours in engine stylesheets with those tokens and delete dead rules. The `.choice`, `.learning-option`, `.vocab-choice` and `.reading-tile` buttons should become one component.

### 4. Too much adult text, in the wrong places (medium)
Course pages and play screens carry long caveats ("Curriculum draft · Educator review pending…", "not assessed", "no recorded voice") in several places at once. The play screen still ends with two or three adult panels ("Why we're playing", "Course: …", "Adult model & adaptations"). **Recommendation:** one collapsible "For the adult" drawer per screen, with evidence limits written once on the course page. Avoid tiny 11–12px grey text; much of it fails comfortable reading size.

### 5. Catalogue shape (medium)
46 courses, 20 of which have only 2–3 activities, and 24 in "Letters, sounds & early reading" alone. Parents face many near-identical choices. **Recommendation:** merge the 3-activity reading courses into 3–4 staged paths, like the existing 16-activity "v2" pilots, and keep the rest as "extra practice" inside those paths.

### 6. The "Your choice" drop box on simple picture choices (medium)
Vocabulary and support-pathway choice screens put a large dashed "Your choice — drag a picture here, or tap one below" box above the pictures. For a one-tap choice this adds an extra concept and pushes the pictures down. **Recommendation:** make the picture's own pressed state the feedback, and keep drop targets only where placing something is the point (baskets, slots, scenes).

### 7. Illustration consistency (medium)
- The same few scenes (robot workshop, house and shop, bare classroom) are reused as thumbnails for many unrelated courses. That makes courses look alike and the pictures meaningless.
- Some support-area pictures don't say what the area is: an empty room with a bed for "Calm & movement", and an easel for "Motor access".
- Scene figures are very small inside large flat backgrounds.

**Recommendation:** give each support area and each course path its own illustration, built with the shared generators and checked with `npm run test:art`.

### 8. Documentation drift (low)
`README.md` (290 lines) is a change log rather than a guide. Parts of it contradict the app, for example "No … dragging is required" next to sections describing dragging. Several docs have missing spaces ("activity3", "remain16"). **Recommendation:** a short README (what it is, how to run, how to test, folder map), with history moved to `CHANGELOG.md`.

### 9. Mobile home (low)
On phones the four-item bottom navigation is fixed over content and the first support cards render at about 120px wide. A single-column card list with larger pictures would read better. The hero also pushes the support cards below the first screen.

## Part 3 — Second pass: items 1–7

### 1. Test suites (done)
All 24 Playwright suites pass again, and `npm test` now runs 33 commands. They're a mix of browser and DOM suites, and include the art and canal checks that were missing from it.
- **App bugs that broke tests.** `?library=full` and `?library=playroom` (documented free-play routes) rendered the home page instead of the library. Inside those libraries their own navigation was hidden. Both are fixed by the router (item 2) and a library mode with a "← Main app" link.
- **Canal Rescue bug.** At the landing, the docked boat and the cargo aboard were drawn over the landing, so tapping the landing to unload Duck selected the parcel or boat instead. While an object is being moved, other movable scene objects now let taps through unless they are a valid destination. Pump and gate stay tappable (`purposeful-play.css`).
- **Stale expectations updated** to the current product: the Courses page instead of the removed home "skills" toggle, the shared Anywhere Adventure Bus where the retired topic picker was, research section order, nav labels, and taps instead of drags into removed drop boxes. The retired topic-picker checks became regression checks that it stays gone.
- Five suites hard-coded `/usr/bin/chromium`; all suites now honour `CHROMIUM_PATH`.
- `.github/workflows/tests.yml` runs `check`, a Prettier check and the whole suite on every push and pull request, so this cannot silently rot again. *The workflow has not run yet: it needs a push to GitHub.*

### 2. Formatting and one router (done; ES-module port not attempted)
- All hand-written JS/CSS/tests/scripts are formatted with Prettier (`npm run format`), about 26,500 readable lines instead of 2,000-character lines. Generated data files are excluded.
- **Prettier 3.3.3 has a bug** that deletes elements of nested arrays inside template literals; it silently damaged the orange, strawberry and other drawings. The repo pins 3.6.2. The formatting was redone from the original sources and verified with `prettier --debug-check` (output AST identical to input).
- `npm run stamp` regenerates the `?v=` cache stamps from file hashes, and `npm run check` fails if they're stale.
- `dist/router.js` is now the only routing listener. Before, one Back press could render up to three times (legacy popstate, Studio popstate and Studio hashchange), and the legacy startup restore could overwrite Studio routes. It deduplicates events, tracks pushState, restores the URL the visitor opened, and dispatches library routes.
- **Not done: converting 60 classic scripts to ES modules.** They share top-level `const`/`let` through the global lexical scope and override each other's functions, so a module port means an explicit import graph for every file. That is a project in itself; formatting, one router and passing tests make it practical to do engine by engine.

### 3. Colours and one picture-choice button (done)
- `scripts/consolidate-colours.py` snapped every hard-coded colour within CIEDE2000 distance 5 of a design token to that token: 681 of 1,044 colour uses. 339 one-off values remain; most are illustration tints in scene CSS.
- One shared picture-choice component covers `.choice`, `.learning-option`, `.vocabulary-choice`, world pictures and all `data-*-choice` buttons. It has the same shape and the same rest, hover, focus, selected, hinted, correct, wrong and disabled states in every engine.

### 4. No more "Your choice" drop box on one-tap choices (done)
Single-picture choices in the vocabulary, support-pathway, focus and calm engines show the choice on the pressed picture itself. Drop targets remain where placing is the point: the model picture, baskets, slots and scenes. Vocabulary picture buttons now expose `aria-pressed`.

### 5. Reading paths (done)
The 20 reading courses with 2–3 activities are four staged paths: First letters and sounds (15 activities), Blend and build short words (17), Practise reading short words (15), and Reading in play and plans (12). Each original course is one stage. The catalogue lists 30 courses instead of 46. The old courses keep their routes and saved progress, and progress recorded under an old course shows as explored in the path. Support areas link to the paths (`dist/reading-paths-data.js`).

### 6. One adult panel per play screen (done)
In a course, the "Why we're playing" card is the single adult panel: one visible sentence plus the course goal. Every other adult block that engines scattered around the game (tips, teaching notes, observation forms, "try it away from the screen", course context) is moved into it as a collapsed row. Panel text is at least 14–15px.

### 7. A picture for every course and support area (done)
`dist/design-covers.js` composes each cover from three objects belonging to that course, plus a motif showing how it works: letter tiles, a numbered sequence, a matching tick, a plan list, a choice ring, steps or beats. Each of the 30 listed courses and 8 support areas has a distinct cover. There are no figures, so no new anatomy is drawn; on the steps covers the cards rest on the steps. All objects are existing vocabulary drawings.

### Verification for this pass
- Automated: every suite in `npm test`, plus `test:studio`, `test:device` (normal, blocked and corrupt storage), `test:reading`, `test:purposeful`, `test:art` and `npm run check`, was run on a snapshot of the final tree.
- Visual review (screenshots looked at by a person): all 38 covers on a contact sheet, plus the courses page, a path page, a support page, four adult panels, six engines' choice buttons, and one-tap choice screens before and after a tap.
- Not verified: physical touch devices, real screen readers, and the GitHub Actions workflow (not yet run).

## Suggested order of work
1. Fix or retire the stale browser suites (item 1), so later work has a safety net.
2. Format the code; introduce the module and router skeleton (item 2).
3. Move colours to tokens and unify the choice-button component (item 3), and remove the "Your choice" box from single-tap screens (item 6).
4. Consolidate the reading courses into paths (item 5) and move adult text into one drawer per screen (item 4).
5. Create course and support-area illustrations (item 7); rewrite the README (item 8).
