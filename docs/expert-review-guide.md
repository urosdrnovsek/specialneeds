# Spot & Match: expert review guide

Open **My practice → Expert review workspace**, or use:
https://spot-and-match-visual-course.blossomingchip1.chatgpt.site/#review

The workspace contains 48 placements in three draft pilot courses, 30 abstract-based article notes and 15 journal candidate records. A placement may reuse an existing game; these are not 48 unique game systems. The three pilots currently include 33 interactive placements and 15 shared activities away from the screen.

## Suggested order

1. Choose **Everyday words and useful messages**. Review the goal and entry knowledge, open an activity in a new tab and try its actions. Check picture meanings, vocabulary, the adult model, task-specific access demands and whether the real-world transfer makes sense.
2. Choose **Read short words and connect them with meaning**. Check the spelling sequence, picture names and distractors. Six placements use rotating pools: 16 short words, eight two-word pairs and 12 two-item lists. Each visit uses four items; the adult should introduce all necessary vocabulary and spellings. Repeated visits rotate content, not difficulty, and do not establish mastery.
3. Choose **Read a short message and make something happen**. Check that the printed message explains the child's meaningful action. Identify helper words, language demands, counting or motor demands that could mask comprehension.
4. Review article summaries against the source abstracts, then full text where needed. Check participant descriptions, design, findings, limitations and the relationship claimed to activities. The current notes are AI-prepared abstract-only drafts; no independent approval is implied.
5. Review journal credentials. Verify the metric year and official database coverage. Two records have publisher-reported metadata; 13 remain pending. None should gain a verified badge solely because a DOI or publisher page exists. Inspect the flagged Donolato correction and any related claims.

## Record a useful decision

Use **Ready for my intended use**, **Changes needed**, **Not relevant to my review**, or leave **Not reviewed**. In notes, identify the exact word, instruction, picture relationship, claim or adaptation that needs attention. Include your intended age/language/support context where relevant, without child-identifying details.

Changes save locally as you type. Export your review before changing devices or clearing browser storage. Import merges recognised review IDs, keeps newer local notes and rejects invalid entries. A changed content fingerprint returns the record to pending while retaining the notes. Local reviewer decisions do not automatically change published editorial labels.

**Download content pack** exports a snapshot of all 93 records with fingerprints. **Export my review** exports your decisions and notes separately from practice data. The three pilot course pages also offer detailed plain-text practice plans.
